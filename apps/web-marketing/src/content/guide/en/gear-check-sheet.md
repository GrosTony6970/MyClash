_Print this page and leave it at the gear check._

## 1. Sign in

Open the link your organiser gave you, or scan its QR code. Type your **Username** and your
**PIN**, then tap **Sign in with PIN**. The **Gear check** screen opens.

## 2. Find the fighter

Type at least two letters of their name in **Search a name**. Under the name, there is one line
per weapon, taken from the tournaments they entered. A weapon not yet checked shows **Not
checked**.

## 3. Record the result

On the weapon's line, tap:

- **Pass**: the equipment is fine;
- **Conditional**: it can be used once something is fixed. Type what must be fixed (it is
  required), then tap **Save conditional**;
- **Fail**: the equipment is refused. There is no field for the reason: tell the fighter, and the
  organiser.

## 4. Fix a mistake

There is no undo. Tap **Re-check** on the weapon, then the right result. The latest result is
the one that counts; the earlier ones stay in the history.

## 5. The tabs

**All**, **To check**, **Pass**, **Conditional**, **Fail**, each with a count. A fighter with
several weapons sits under their worst result: **Fail**, then **Conditional**, then **To check**.

## 6. Good to know

- _The result never stops a bout._ The scoring tablet shows it beside the fighter's name
  (**Gear**, with the reason of a conditional), so the referee can see it. Whether a fighter with
  **Fail** fights is the organiser's and the referee's decision.
- _It needs the network._ Without it, a tap shows **That did not save. Try again.**: nothing is
  kept for later. Write the results on paper and enter them when the network comes back.
- **Log out** asks before it signs you out.

## Call the organiser when

- a weapon is refused (**Fail**);
- a fighter shows **No weapon resolved for this fighter — check their tournament entries.**;
- someone is not on the list, or the screen says **Wrong account for this screen**.
