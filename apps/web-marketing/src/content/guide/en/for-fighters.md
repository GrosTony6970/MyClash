## For the organiser: before you send this page

- **Publish the event, as a Standard or a Club event.** At a test event or a draft, fighters see a
  page that is not found, even for their own schedule.
- **Enter each fighter's email** (in the import or by hand). A fighter claims their profile with
  that email.
- **You register fighters for the tournaments.** Fighters cannot do it themselves. They can book
  a place in a workshop themselves, once they are on the participants list.
- **You can email each fighter their pass:** on **Participants**, **Email passes** sends one to
  everyone who has an email and no MyClash account. _Editor_ or above.
- Send them your event's address: `app.myclash.fr/e/<your event>`.

The rest of this page is written for your fighters.

---

## Follow the event

Open the event's address on your phone. The tournaments, the pools, the schedule, the live bouts
and the results are there, with no account. While the event runs, **Live now** shows what is
being fought on each piste.

## Your schedule

On the event page, under **Taking part in this event?**, tap **Find my name**, then your name, then
**This is me!**. Two choices:

- **Quick access without an account:** your schedule, your pass, following other fighters and
  booking workshops, on this phone only, until a week after the event. **End guest session**
  ends it.
- **Claim my profile:** links you to your MyClash account (see below).

**My schedule** lists your bouts with the piste, the opponent and the score, your refereeing
duties and your workshops, and warns about clashes. **Focus on me** keeps only what is yours. The
phone keeps a copy for when the signal drops.

## Your pass for the welcome desk

**My event pass**, linked from your schedule, is a QR code: "Show this at the check-in desk." It
is kept on your phone, so it shows without signal. Opening it on another phone makes a new pass,
and the old one stops working.

## A MyClash account

1. Open `app.myclash.fr/login`, tab **Sign up**, and enter your email. Open the link you receive
   and choose a password (or use **Send magic link instead**).
2. To link your profile at this event: on your name, **This is me!**, then **Claim my profile**.
   Enter **Your registered email**, the one the organiser has, and tap **Send confirmation link**.
   The link works for one hour. If the email does not match, ask the organiser to check your
   registration.

If you sign up with exactly the email the organiser entered, your profile links by itself. With an
account, your schedule follows you on every device, and you get notifications.

## Notifications

They need an account. In your space, **Enable notifications**, then choose what you want: a
reminder before a bout, a refereeing duty or a workshop, **Schedule changes**, **Results
published**, and more. They arrive as phone notifications, or by email if you have not enabled
them.

## Workshops

On a workshop's page, **Register** books a place; when it is full, **Join waitlist**. **Cancel
registration** gives the place back. One session per workshop.

## Follow other fighters

On a participant's page, **Follow**: with an account, you are told when they fight. To stop
others following you, turn off **Allow others to follow me** in your settings.

## What others see about you

- The public participants list shows your name, your club, your tournaments and your HEMA rating.
  _Never your email._
- On your fighter profile, you choose what is public (**Public visibility**). **Appear in the
  public fighter directory** is on by default; **Let search engines index my profile** is off.

## On your phone

The language switch (FR / EN) is at the top of the site. To install MyClash as an app: on Android,
**Install the app** at the bottom of the page; on an iPhone, Safari's **Share** menu, then **Add to
Home Screen**.
