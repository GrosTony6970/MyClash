_Print this page and leave it by the tablet._

## 1. Sign in

Open the link your organiser gave you, or scan its QR code. Type your **Username** and your
**PIN**, then tap **Sign in with PIN**. Tap your piste, then the bout under **NEXT**.

## 2. Run the bout

- **Start** starts the clock (or press the space bar).
- The referee calls halt: tap **Pause**.
- _Enter the exchange while the clock is paused_ (the buttons are off while it runs), in the
  column of the fighter who hit:
  - **Clean hit**: the value the referee gives (+2, +1…);
  - **Afterblow**: 2-1, 1-1…;
  - **Double**: a double hit (key D);
  - **No exchange**: nothing valid, then pick the reason.
- **Resume** restarts the clock.

## 3. Fix a mistake

**Undo last entry** takes back the last thing you entered (key U). It works even without wifi.
For anything older, call the organiser.

## 4. Cards and protests

- **Penalties**: tap a penalty, or type in **Search penalties**. The pad shows the card the
  fighter gets. A red or a black card asks you to confirm.
- The hand button counts a fighter's protests. Past the limit, the pad offers the penalty: confirm
  only if the referee gives it.

## 5. End the bout

With the network, the bout ends by itself when a hit reaches the point cap. When time is up, or
without network, tap **End match**; check the score first. The result screen then offers
**NEXT** for the next bout.

## 6. The wifi drops

The bar at the top turns grey: **OFFLINE - exchanges queued locally**. _Keep going._ Everything
you enter is sent when the network comes back. Only the corrections and reopening an ended bout
need the network: they are refused until it comes back.

## Call the organiser when

- you made a mistake older than the last entry;
- a bout must be reopened, or the pad says it is locked;
- the bar turns red (**SYNC ERROR**, or hits not recorded).
