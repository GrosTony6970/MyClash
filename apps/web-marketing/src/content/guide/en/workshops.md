**What you'll learn:** how to add workshops to an event, open them to sign-ups, and manage the
people who book.

## Before you start

- Every event has a **Workshops** page in its menu. There is nothing to turn on.
- An instructor is a person of your event. On the **Participants** page, tick **Also tag as
  instructor for this event** on that person. The person needs a MyClash profile.
- To create and change workshops, you need the **Instructor** role in your organisation, or a
  higher one.

## Create a workshop

1. Open **Workshops**, then click **+ New workshop**.
2. Type the **Title**. The **Slug** fills itself in from the title. Two workshops of one event
   cannot have the same slug.
3. Set the **Capacity**: the number of places in each session. The form starts at 20.
4. Under **Sessions**, click **Add a session** for each time slot: the day, the start and end
   times, and the **Session venue**.
5. Tick the **Instructors**. Only the people tagged as instructors are in the list. After you
   save, **Details for this workshop** sets the bio, club and photo shown for this workshop.
6. Leave the **Status** on **Draft** while you prepare, then click **Create**.

The other fields (weapon, level, language, logo, descriptions, prerequisites) are for the public
page.

**Capacity is per session.** A workshop with a capacity of 20 and three sessions has 20 places
in each session.

The **Workshop schedule** tab shows every session on a grid. Drag a workshop onto the grid to add
a session. A card turns red when one of its instructors teaches, referees or fights at the same
time. This is a warning only: the move is saved.

## Open the sign-ups

Set the status to **Published**, in the form or in the status list of the workshop's row. The
statuses are **Draft**, **Published**, **Running** and **Completed**. People can book a
published or running workshop. The public sees no draft workshop, and nothing of a Draft or Test
event.

- **Who can book:** the people on your event's list, with a MyClash account or a guest session
  on their phone (see [For your fighters](/en/guide/for-fighters)).
- **People may join this Event by themselves:** with this switch on, a person with a MyClash
  account who is not on your list can add themselves, then book. Only an **Admin** can change it.
  Other members see the switch, and their save is refused.
- **One place per workshop:** a person holds one session of a workshop at a time. A waitlist
  place counts as that place.
- **The waitlist:** when a session is full, a new booking goes on the waitlist. When a place
  frees up, the first person on the waitlist gets it at once. MyClash tells them if they have an
  account.
- **No payment:** MyClash takes no money for a workshop.

## The roster

**Roster** lists the people booked, session by session.

- **Add a person:** books someone already on your event's list. A full session puts them on the
  waitlist. It also works on a draft workshop, so you can prepare the list before you publish.
- **Promote:** gives a place to someone on the waitlist.
- **Remove:** deletes the booking. The person can book again.
- **Present:** a tick for the people who came. It changes nothing else.
- **Message the people booked:** writes to everyone booked.

An instructor sees the roster of their own workshops in their personal space, even with no role
in your organisation. There they can also **Accept** or **Refuse** a booking.

## What MyClash refuses

- Deleting a workshop while someone holds a place or a waitlist place. Remove the bookings first.
- An instructor booking a workshop they teach.
- Booking, promoting or moving in a cancelled session.

## Workshops and refereeing

Nobody referees while they teach a workshop at the same time. No setting changes this. Refereeing
while attending a workshop is only discouraged: the **Attending a Workshop** rule on the
**Referees** page can switch it off (see [Staff and referees](/en/guide/staff-referees)).
