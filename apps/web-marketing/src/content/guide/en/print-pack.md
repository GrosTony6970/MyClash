**What you'll learn:** what the print pack holds, and why you print it before the event.

## Why paper

The scoring tablets keep working without the network (see
[Scorekeeper sheet](/en/guide/scorekeeper-sheet)). The print pack is for the day the app
itself cannot be reached at all: you run the bouts on paper and enter the results afterwards.

**Print it before the event.** The page reads everything from the server, so it needs the network.
Keep the sheets at each piste.

## Print it

1. On the event's overview, open the **Print pack** card.
2. If the event has several tournaments, choose one under **Tournament**, or **All Tournaments, in
   one print job**.
3. Under **What to print**, tick the sheets you want.
4. Click **Print**. The pack opens in a new window, and the print dialog opens by itself. If nothing
   opens, allow pop-ups for the site.

Every member of your organisation can print the pack.

## The sheets

All sheets are A4, one per page. Each header names the event, the tournament, the ruleset and the
time it was printed, on the event's clock.

| Sheet                 | Ticked at first | What it holds                                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pool sheets**       | Yes             | One page per pool: the fighters, then every bout with Score, Doubles, Penalties and Winner, and a line to sign |
| **Bracket sheet**     | Yes             | Every bracket bout, round by round, with a score box for each side and its piste                               |
| **Piste day-sheets**  | Yes             | One page per piste: its bouts in time order, with the referees. Bouts with no piste go on a page of their own  |
| **Match scoresheets** | No              | One page per bout: 20 numbered exchange rows, Penalties and Notes, Winner and a line to sign                   |

## What it leaves out

- **Swiss bouts:** the pack holds pools and brackets only.
- **A tournament with no pools and no bracket yet:** there is nothing to print. With **All
  Tournaments**, such a tournament is left out of the job.
- **Two fighters with the same name** in one pool show as one line in the pool's fighter list.
