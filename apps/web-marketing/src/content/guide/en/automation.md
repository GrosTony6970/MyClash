**What you'll learn:** what MyClash does without you, what sets it off, and what it always leaves
to you.

## What MyClash does for you

| What                                                                                               | When                                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Draw the pools, keeping clubs apart and spreading the strongest                                    | When you click **Generate pools**                                                             |
| Propose time blocks for each phase, then place every bout on a piste and a time                    | When you click **Generate schedule**, then **Generate Grid**                                  |
| Propose a referee for every role                                                                   | When you click **Preview auto assign**; nothing changes until you click **Apply auto assign** |
| End a bout when a hit reaches the point cap (in pools and Swiss rounds, also at the doubles limit) | When that hit reaches MyClash (without network, the scorekeeper ends it)                      |
| End a bout when a hit reaches the point cap (in pools and Swiss rounds, also at the doubles limit) | When that hit reaches MyClash (without network, the scorekeeper ends it)                      |
| Move a bout's winner to the next round                                                             | When the bout ends                                                                            |
| Fill the bracket from the pool standings                                                           | When the last pool bout ends, if the bracket is generated                                     |
| Pair the next Swiss round                                                                          | When the last bout of the round ends                                                          |
| Keep every standing up to date                                                                     | All the time: standings are calculated each time they are shown                               |
| Lock a finished pool or bracket against changes                                                    | 15 minutes after its last bout (on by default; see the tournament's **Auto-lock** tab)        |
| Set a published event to running, then completed                                                   | At midnight on its first day, then at 6:00 the morning after its last day                     |
| Remind fighters, referees and followers before their bout, duty or workshop                        | Before it starts                                                                              |
| Tell fighters their Swiss pairing                                                                  | When a round is paired                                                                        |
| Tell your followers about a new event                                                              | The first time you publish a Standard event                                                   |
| Recalculate league standings                                                                       | When the event is completed, or a hit or card of a finished event is corrected                |

Auto-assign never picks a referee that a rule warns about. It leaves the slot empty and says why,
so you can decide by hand.

**Start of day** checks that you are ready (the event, the roster, the draw, the schedule). It
warns; it never stops you.

## What stays your decision

- Publishing the event and each tournament, and setting them back to draft.
- Generating the pools and the bracket, and regenerating them.
- Applying and locking the referee assignments.
- Ending a bout on time or before the point cap: the scorekeeper clicks **End match**.
- Marking a tournament as completed, which sends the results to its fighters.
- Sending the results to HEMA Ratings.
