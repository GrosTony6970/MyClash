**What you'll learn:** what the debrief shows, and how to collect what people thought of your
event.

## Open it

Open **Debrief** in the event's menu. You need the **Scorekeeper** role or higher. You can open it
at any time, not only after the event.

The headline says **Nothing needs your attention.** or **Some things need a look.**

## The six cards

Each card shows a count, always, even at zero.

| Card                               | What it counts                                                                                                                 | Needs a look when    |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| **Exchanges the server refused**   | Hits a tablet scored that never reached the server, counted at their highest: a tablet that recovered still shows what it lost | Any                  |
| **Devices that stopped reporting** | Tablets that went silent more than 10 minutes before the last one did. What they held is unknown                               | Any                  |
| **Recorded overrides**             | Results changed by hand, by reason                                                                                             | Never: a normal tool |
| **Overrides later voided**         | Overrides that were undone                                                                                                     | Never                |
| **Arrivals recorded at the desk**  | People marked present, by name search or by scanned pass                                                                       | Never                |
| **Devices with a drifting clock**  | Tablet accounts whose clock is off by 30 seconds or more                                                                       | Any                  |

**Open the full clock report** shows the clocks in detail.

A tablet switched off early in the day shows under **Devices that stopped reporting**: check that it
was only switched off.

## What people said

The panel **What people said** shows the average rating out of 5, the number of answers, a line per
role (fighters, referees, instructors, attendees) and the comments.

- A role with fewer than 3 answers is not shown on its own line.
- A comment is anonymous unless the person ticked **Sign this feedback: the organiser will see your
  name.**
- An answer given again replaces the first one.

**MyClash does not ask for the feedback.** Share the form yourself: its address is your event's
public page followed by `/feedback`, for example `app.myclash.fr/e/your-event/feedback`. Anyone
can answer, with an account or as a guest on their phone.
