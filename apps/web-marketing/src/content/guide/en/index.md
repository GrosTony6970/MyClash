This guide is for organisers who are new to MyClash. It follows one event from start to finish,
step by step: from creating your account to publishing the results.

Just want to try it out? Create a **Test event**. Nobody outside your club can see it, it counts
in no statistics, and you can delete it whenever you like. Experiment freely: that is what it is
for.

The journey:

1. Before you start: your account, and which kind of event to choose
2. Create the event
3. Add a tournament
4. Register the fighters
5. Make the pools
6. Build the schedule
7. Staff and referees
8. Event day: the scoring pad
9. The bracket
10. The final ranking and the results

Then two more sections:

- **Understand MyClash**: how it works, the formats, what it does by itself, rulesets, and staff
  access. Read it once you have run a test event.
- **Scoring pad**: a sheet to print and leave by each tablet, and the full reference of the pad
  for you, to train your scorekeepers.
