_Print this page and leave it at the welcome desk._

## 1. Sign in

Open the link your organiser gave you, or scan its QR code. Type your **Username** and your
**PIN**, then tap **Sign in with PIN**. The **Check-in** screen opens.

## 2. Check someone in

1. Type at least two letters of their name in **Search a name**.
2. Check the photo and the club (**No club** if they have none).
3. Tap **Arrived**. The row now shows the time they arrived.

Wrong person? Tap **Undo** on their row. On the second day of an event, someone who arrived the
day before shows **Arrived today**: tap it when they come back.

## 3. Scan a pass

Fighters can show **My event pass**, a QR code, on their phone. Tap **Scan QR** and point the
camera at it. _A good scan checks the person in at once_, with no button to confirm: check the
name that appears, and tap **Undo** if it is the wrong one. **Desk** takes you back to the list.

If the pass is refused ("That pass is not for this event.", "That pass has expired.") or the
camera does not work, use **Search by name instead**.

## 4. The tabs

- **All**, **Arrived**, **Not arrived**, each with a count.
- **Not arrived** lists first the people who fight soonest, with the time, the tournament and the
  piste, or **Not scheduled yet**. Tell the organiser about anyone who fights soon and is not
  there.

Two tablets can work the same desk: each list reloads after every tap, so the other tablet's
check-ins appear too.

## 5. Good to know

- _Checking in blocks nothing._ Someone who was not checked in can still fight. The desk only
  tells the organiser who is there.
- _It needs the network._ Without it, the list does not load and a tap shows **That did not save.
  Try again.**: nothing is kept for later. Write the names on paper and enter them when the
  network comes back.
- **Log out** asks before it signs you out.

## Call the organiser when

- someone is not on the list (the organiser adds them);
- the screen says **Wrong account for this screen**;
- the sign-in is refused, or the list stays empty with a red message.
