**What you'll learn:** how people send photos to your event, how you check them, and what the
public sees.

## Turn them on

Open **Photos** in the event's menu (you need the **Admin** role). Under **Who sees the photos**:

| Choice            | What happens                                                  |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Off**           | Nobody can send photos, and none are shown. This is the start |
| **Approve first** | Each photo is shown once a moderator approves it              |
| **Live**          | Each photo is shown as soon as it arrives                     |

A photo keeps the state it arrived with. A photo sent under **Approve first** still waits after you
switch to **Live**. Switching to **Off** deletes nothing.

## Who can send

- **Anyone with the QR code**, with no account. The name, the caption and the tag (a tournament or
  a workshop) are all optional. **Print poster** prints the QR code; **Copy link** copies its
  link. **New link** replaces the QR code: every poster already printed stops working.
- **A person with an account**, from their personal space, for an event in their list. Their photo
  carries their name.

Sending is open from 00:00 on the day before the event until the end of the day after it, on the
event's clock. At a Draft or Test event, a phone without a member's account cannot open the send
page.

**The rules for a photo:** JPEG only, up to 12 MB. An iPhone photo in HEIC is refused, with a tip to
set the camera to "Most Compatible". Some photos exported from an editing app are refused too: send
the photo straight from the camera. Each phone can send 20 photos an hour. The location data in a
photo is removed.

## Check them

Three places can approve and delete:

- the **Photos** page of the event (an **Admin**);
- a **Photos** tablet account (see [Staff access](/en/guide/staff-access)), until the event is
  archived;
- the MyClash team.

The tabs are **Waiting**, **Reported** and **All**. **Approve** shows a waiting photo. **Delete**
removes a photo for good: there is no "reject", Delete is the reject. **Keep** answers the reports
on a reported photo and leaves it shown.

**Nobody is notified** of a new or a reported photo: look at the counts in the tabs.

## What the public sees

With **Live** or **Approve first**, the event's page shows a **Photos** card that opens the
approved photos, newest first, with their name, caption and tag. Search engines do not list this
page.

Anyone can **Report** a photo, with an optional comment. The photo stays shown until a moderator
decides.

**A person in a photo who wants it gone** reports it with a comment, or asks you. A person who sent
a photo from their personal space can delete it there at any time. A photo sent with the QR code
can only be deleted by a moderator.

## How long they stay

Every photo, with its reports, is deleted on the 31st day after the event's last day. The Photos
page shows the date. Moving the event's end date moves it too. Until then, an archived event still
shows its photos, takes reports and can be moderated.

**Download zip** (an **Admin**) downloads the waiting and approved photos, with a list of them.
