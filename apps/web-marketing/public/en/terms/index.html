  • You are the data controller for that roster, within the meaning of the GDPR. MyClash acts as your processor, holding and processing that data on your instructions.
  • You warrant that you have a lawful basis for entering those people's data — typically their registration for your event — and that you have informed them of it.
  • You undertake to relay requests: a person on your roster who asks for access, correction or erasure must be able to obtain it from you. We will help; we cannot answer in your place for data you control.

  • You do not use these rosters for anything else — no reuse for prospecting, no transfer to a third party, no purpose other than running your event.
