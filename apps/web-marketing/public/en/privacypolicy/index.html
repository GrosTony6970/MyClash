7. Cookies and similar technologies

The MyClash application uses cookies and similar technologies. In accordance with CNIL 2025-2026 requirements and the ePrivacy reform (Digital Omnibus Package), our cookie management respects the following principles:

  • No dark patterns: refusing non-essential cookies is as easy and accessible as accepting them.
  • Consent granularity: each processing purpose has a separate consent option — no forced bundling.
  • No conditional access: access to the application is not subject to accepting non-essential cookies.

9. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or destruction, in accordance with the CNIL's enhanced requirements (December 2025):

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest
  • Password hashing (bcrypt or equivalent)
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) mandatory for all access to critical systems and databases
  • Access logging: any connection to personal data is traced (who, when, which data) to detect abnormal behaviour
  • Sovereign infrastructure: exclusive hosting on OVHcloud and Scaleway (French data centres)
  • Error monitoring via self-hosted Sentry, with no data exposure to third parties
  • Data access restricted to authorised personnel, based on the principle of least privilege
  • Incident response procedure in case of a data breach, with notification to the CNIL within 72 hours (Art. 33 GDPR)
