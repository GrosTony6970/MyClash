# ADR-006 — No paid sponsorship or branding on the platform at v1

**Date:** 2025-01-01
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Community platforms often face pressure to monetise via sponsor logos, banner ads, or paid placement. MyClash is volunteer-built for the HEMA community. Introducing commercial sponsorship at v1 would:

- Complicate the neutral positioning of the platform
- Require legal agreements before the platform is even stable
- Risk alienating clubs that associate the platform with commercial interests

## Decision

**No paid sponsorship, banner advertising, or branded placement at v1.**

Event organisers may include their own club/event logos via per-event theming — this is not sponsorship, it is personalisation.

Funding is via voluntary donations only (HelloAsso / Lyon AMHE association).

## Consequences

- **Easy:** Platform UI is clean and community-focused. No legal agreements to manage.
- **Hard:** Revenue is limited to voluntary donations; infrastructure costs covered by the project owner.
- **Committed to:** If sponsorship is introduced in a future version, it requires an explicit ADR superseding this one and community consultation.

## Alternatives considered

- **Affiliate/sponsor banners** — Rejected as inconsistent with the free/neutral mission.
- **Premium organiser plans** — Deferred to a potential v2 discussion; contradicts "free for organisers" commitment at v1.
